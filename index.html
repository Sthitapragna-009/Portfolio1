Profile

Design, built the way it's engineered.

I'm an industrial designer working out of IIT Roorkee's Department of Design, where design briefs rarely stay on one medium. A project might start as a research question about how people use a water motor, and end as an interface with states, thresholds, and edge cases — or start as a century-old coffee roastery's story and end as a storefront.

Most of what's here started as an unofficial redesign or a personal case study — a way of testing ideas against real briefs, real constraints, and real content, not just a moodboard.
